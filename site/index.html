Your music library, looked after.

Codetta fixes tags, matches albums on MusicBrainz and files new downloads where they belong, from any browser. On your own server, or your own computer.

Looks after the music you already have.

Tidy up goes through your library one album at a time. It fixes tags from MusicBrainz, adds missing covers, and files every album with your folder pattern. You see a preview first, it only changes what it's sure about, and every album can be undone.
